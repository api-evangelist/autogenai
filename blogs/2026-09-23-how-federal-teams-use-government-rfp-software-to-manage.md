A complex federal RFP contains dozens of requirements across Section L, Section M, the PWS, SOW, attachments, referenced documents, and amendments. Tracking them manually across multiple documents and contributors can create compliance risk before drafting even begins. Purpose-built AI for federal proposals changes that process.
